Textbook source setup

Choose the local folder containing your authorized CPM textbook mirrors. Nothing is uploaded or sent to a cloud server.

Scope for pilot

All seven CPM courses, Chapters 1–6. Every available student lesson is indexed; the cards are extracted on demand with their original problem numbers, notes, and images. Missing chapters are displayed as missing, never filled with placeholders.

For other teachers, they must have their own authorized local textbook mirror. No full copyrighted textbook files are redistributed in this application ZIP.
