What We Need to Know

Slope, Motion & Comparing Rates
Learning focus | LG 02-ican-5 / LG 03-ican-3 / LG 03-ican-4

• I can calculate slope from two points or a table.

• I can interpret a slope using units and a direction.

• I can compare two rates fairly, including negative slopes.

Vocabulary | Highlight the important words

Rate of changeHow much an output changes compared with an input change, with units attached.
SlopeThe vertical change divided by the horizontal change of a straight line.
Positive / negative slopeA positive slope rises as x increases; a negative slope falls as x increases.
Zero slopeA horizontal line with no vertical change; slope zero.
PositionAn object's location measured relative to a chosen reference point.
Velocity on a position-time graphThe rate and direction of change in position; the slope is signed and measured in distance per time.

Meaning & Method | Rewritten review notes

1. Always find slope with a ratio: (change in output) / (change in input), not the vertical change alone.

2. In context, read the sign and the units. A negative position-time slope can mean travel toward the chosen zero position; it does not mean negative speed.

3. To compare steepness or speed, compare magnitudes when the units are the same; explain the signs separately as direction or decreasing quantity.

Review & Preview

1 ★ Position goes from 32 m at 0 s to 14 m at 6 s. Calculate average rate of change with units.
ANSWER / TEACHING NOTE

(14 - 32)/(6 - 0) = -18/6 = -3 m/s.

2 Find the slope between (-2,7) and (4,-5). Show Delta y and Delta x.
ANSWER / TEACHING NOTE

(-5 - 7)/(4 - (-2)) = -12/6 = -2.

3 ★ Two cyclists travel 30 m in 5 s and 42 m in 7 s. Compare their average rates.
ANSWER / TEACHING NOTE

Both are 6 m/s: 30/5 = 6 and 42/7 = 6.
