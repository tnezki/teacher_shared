Teacher & Student Discourse Moves

Generic moves · not tied to an I Can or lesson
↑ Resource Home

Projected Question & Solution

Teacher moves

Ask for a prediction or justification before revealing the solution.

Student moves

Try a method, show thinking, then correct with evidence.

Standard Worksheet / Notability

Teacher moves

Circulate for reasoning and target misconceptions rather than checking completion alone.

Student moves

Show legible steps; mark changes after comparing a solution.

Find Someone Who

Teacher moves

Require an explanation before each signature; encourage a variety of partners.

Student moves

Attempt first, then explain the solution before signing each other’s work.

Rounds

Teacher moves

Pause between rounds to compare strategies and decide on one improvement.

Student moves

Check the four questions in each round; explain one change you made.

Stations

Teacher moves

Monitor each group, note different approaches, and bring one comparison to debrief.

Student moves

Record reasoning at each station and help every teammate contribute.

Use alongside any lesson question set or printable.Part 1 of 2

Ambassador

Cross-Pollination
↑ Options · All A–Z
Structure

Study Team and Teaching Strategy

Suggested materials

Projected questions · worksheet · stations

Setup

One representative visits another team.

Directions
  1. Finish and justify the team solution.
  2. Prepare to explain the reasoning, not just the result.
  3. Send an ambassador to support another team by asking questions rather than supplying the answer.
Goal: A finished solution and a reasoned explanation.
Discussion / sentence frames
  • Which idea helped your team move forward?
  • What would you try next, and why?
Looks Like Success
  • Work is legible enough for peers to follow.
  • Every participant contributes or checks reasoning.
  • Groups compare and improve mathematical explanations.
Doesn’t Look Like
  • Students copying rather than explaining.
  • Incomplete work passed on without discussion.
  • Signatures or station rotations without understanding.

Board Report

Cross-Pollination
↑ Options · All A–Z
Structure

Study Team and Teaching Strategy

Suggested materials

Projected questions · board space

Setup

Shared board or projected response space.

Directions
  1. Solve the current problem together.
  2. Send a reporter to display the team response.
  3. Compare posted approaches; discuss and revise as needed.
  4. Repeat with a new problem.
Goal: Teams can post and compare solutions.
Discussion / sentence frames
  • What is similar across the responses?
  • What reasoning supports the difference?
Looks Like Success
  • Everyone makes an attempt before an answer is shown.
  • Students defend their methods using evidence.
  • Mistakes become opportunities to revise.
Doesn’t Look Like
  • Only one student thinking or writing.
  • Answers displayed without discussion.
  • Moving on without checking understanding.

Directions

Standard worksheet / Notability

Setup

One printable worksheet or a Notability copy.

Directions
  1. Work through the selected twelve questions.
  2. Show enough work to explain your strategy.
  3. Check and revise when answers are discussed.
Goal: Flexible written practice.
Discussion / sentence frames
  • How can you justify that?
  • What changed after your discussion?
Looks Like Success
  • Work is legible enough for peers to follow.
  • Every participant contributes or checks reasoning.
  • Groups compare and improve mathematical explanations.
Doesn’t Look Like
  • Students copying rather than explaining.
  • Incomplete work passed on without discussion.
  • Signatures or station rotations without understanding.
