1. Q046 · U1-MG04-IC04

Use \(y=-2x+1\) to complete the table.

x-203
y???
Complete all outputs.

Answer: 5, 1, -5

Short solution: Substitute each input: y(-2)=5, y(0)=1, y(3)=-5.

2. Q008 · U1-MG01-IC03

Use the graph. How much more water is shown at 8 minutes than at 3 minutes?

Show the two readings and the difference.

Answer: 100 L

Short solution: The graph shows 200 L at 8 min and 100 L at 3 min. The difference is 100 L.

3. Q010 · U1-MG01-IC04

Identify the x-intercept and y-intercept of the line.

x-intercept: ______   y-intercept: ______

Answer: x-intercept (3,0); y-intercept (0,6).

Short solution: An x-intercept has y=0; a y-intercept has x=0.

4. Q043 · U1-MG04-IC03

Use the rule \(y=x^2-3\) to complete the table.

xy
-2?
36
Corresponding output: ______

Answer: 1

Short solution: For x=-2, y=(-2)^2-3=4-3=1.

5. Q001 · U1-MG01-IC01

Plot the point \(P(-4,3)\) on the coordinate plane.

Place and label point P.

Answer: P is at (-4, 3).

Short solution: Move 4 units left from the origin and 3 units up; place P there.
