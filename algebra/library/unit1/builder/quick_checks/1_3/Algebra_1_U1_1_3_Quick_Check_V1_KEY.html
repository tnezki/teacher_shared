1. Q043 · U1-MG04-IC03

Use the rule \(y=x^2-3\) to complete the table.

xy
-2?
36
Corresponding output: ______

Answer: 1

Short solution: For x=-2, y=(-2)^2-3=4-3=1.

2. Q119 · U1-MG03-IC03

Evaluate \(-7+3(-4)\).

  1. 5
  2. -5
  3. -19
  4. 19

Answer: C. -19

Short solution: 3(-4)=-12; -7+(-12)=-19.

3. Q106 · U1-MG03-IC01

Evaluate \(2a+3b\) when \(a=-2\) and \(b=5\).

Value: _____

Answer: 11

Short solution: 2(-2)+3(5)=-4+15=11.

4. Q028 · U1-MG03-IC02

Evaluate \(18-3(4)+2^3\).

Value: ______

Answer: 14

Short solution: Exponent: 2^3=8; multiplication: 3(4)=12; then 18-12+8=14.
