OPERATIONAL POLICY
Treat the initial authored content as the working pilot baseline, as directed.
No pause for approval of each question. When a teacher flags an issue, update its
canonical family without renumbering ID. 'Operational' does NOT imply that the
teacher individually proofread all questions.
Do not call early/partial evidence full proficiency.

IMPORTANT SCOPE BOUNDARY
This is an assessment-evidence priority bank, not all lesson source cards and
not yet every lesson's complete question inventory. It has 31 priority I Cans;
the course still has additional lesson-level objectives to populate progressively.
There is no automated parameterized variant generator in this content packet.
